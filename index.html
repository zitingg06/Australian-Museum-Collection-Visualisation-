Treasures of the Commonwealth: Australia's Collecting Story.

An empirical investigation into national heritage collections, commemorative coinage, postal stamp issues, and colonial trade tokens. Driven by clean, structured data from the National Museum of Australia (NMA) Collection API and Royal Australian Mint (RAM) archives spanning 1788 to 2026.

NMA & RAM Collections

Analysing physical objects, materials, production locations, mintages, and historical themes across pre-federation, decimal, and contemporary eras.
